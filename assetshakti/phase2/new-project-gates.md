# AssetShakti Phase 2 — New Project Shakti Gates

These gates are decision controls, not legal certifications.

## P2-G01 Project identity
Project, phase, promoter/entity, location and relevant parcel must be uniquely identified from authoritative or traceable evidence.

## P2-G02 Jurisdiction and regulatory route
Applicable RERA, planning, land-use, municipal, environmental and other statutory routes must be identified for the project's actual jurisdiction and use. Do not assume one approval stack applies everywhere.

## P2-G03 Land/title/rights
Land ownership, development rights, encumbrances, mortgages/charges, leases and material title risks must be evidenced or explicitly marked missing/unverified.

## P2-G04 Approval and disclosure integrity
Material approvals, sanctioned plans, registrations, disclosures and conditions must be traceable to authoritative sources where available. Promotional claims are not treated as regulatory verification.

## P2-G05 Promoter/counterparty
Promoter identity, relevant entity relationships and material disclosed risk signals must be represented with source and observation date.

## P2-G06 Construction/site reality
Construction status, site access, physical progress and material project claims require evidence. A brochure or AI-generated assessment cannot establish physical reality.

## P2-G07 Delivery/contractual risk
Possession commitments, phase dependencies, material conditions, cancellation/payment terms and other buyer obligations must be represented where relevant.

## P2-G08 Economics
Price must be evaluated against comparable evidence and total acquisition cost. Advertised price alone is not investment value.

## P2-G09 Market/liquidity
Market assumptions, comparable evidence, absorption/demand signals and exit assumptions must be distinguished between verified evidence and estimates.

## P2-G10 Contradiction/missing-evidence control
Critical missing, stale or contradictory evidence blocks a ready decision until resolved or explicitly accepted under an approved conditional workflow.

## P2-G11 Decision integrity
No score, AI output or commercial incentive can override a failed critical gate.

## P2-G12 Auditability
Every material assertion must retain source reference, observation date, evidence state and provenance.

## P2-G13 Production certification
Certification requires representative real projects, negative controls, reproducible decisions, risk coverage and documented human review of gate failures.
