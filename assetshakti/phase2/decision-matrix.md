# AssetShakti Phase 2 — New Project Decision Matrix

This matrix is deliberately a validation-stage design. It does not certify real projects.

## Critical evidence domains
1. IDENTITY
2. JURISDICTION
3. TITLE
4. LAND_RIGHTS
5. RERA (where applicable)
6. PLANNING
7. BUILDING_APPROVAL

Additional critical domains become mandatory when the project's use/jurisdiction makes them applicable.

## Decision rule

**DO_NOT_PROCEED**
- Any applicable critical domain is contradicted, or
- material critical evidence is missing where the approved gate requires verification.

**INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE**
- Critical evidence coverage is too incomplete to make a defensible decision.

**CONDITIONAL**
- Critical gates pass but material non-critical uncertainty remains.

**BUY_READY / INVESTMENT_READY**
- Applicable critical gates pass,
- evidence is sufficiently complete,
- contradictions are resolved,
- economics and material risk assumptions are documented,
- and the decision is reproducible.

The exact quantitative thresholds must be validated against the Phase 2 real-world corpus before production use.

## Mandatory principle
A score can summarize evidence. It cannot override a failed critical gate.
