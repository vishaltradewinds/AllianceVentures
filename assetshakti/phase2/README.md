# AssetShakti — Phase 2 New Project Intelligence

Status: Shakti-gated architecture definition — not production certified.

## Purpose
Extend AssetShakti from distressed-property auction intelligence into **new real-estate project intelligence** without weakening the Phase 1 distressed-asset controls.

The Phase 2 operating model evaluates a new residential, commercial, industrial, mixed-use or other applicable real-estate project before a buyer, investor, lender or strategic stakeholder commits capital.

## Core question
**“Is this project sufficiently evidenced, legally/regulatorily viable, physically credible and economically sensible for the intended decision?”**

## Operating boundary
AssetShakti is an independent intelligence and evidence layer. It does not replace RERA authorities, planning/local authorities, courts, professional legal/title advice, registered valuation, engineering certification, environmental approvals, lender diligence or the project's transaction channel.

AI may extract, compare and flag evidence. AI may not certify title, approvals, construction quality, valuation, compliance or investment suitability.

## Project intelligence pipeline
1. Identify project, promoter, land and phase
2. Classify project and intended use
3. Establish jurisdiction and applicable statutory route
4. Capture authoritative evidence
5. Reconcile title, approvals, disclosures and project claims
6. Detect missing, stale and contradictory evidence
7. Assess physical/site/construction reality
8. Assess market and comparable evidence
9. Model buyer/investor economics and total cost
10. Assess delivery, counterparty and exit risks
11. Apply critical Shakti gates
12. Produce an evidence-traceable decision and verification checklist

## Initial decision states
- INVESTMENT_READY
- BUY_READY
- CONDITIONAL
- DO_NOT_PROCEED
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE

A numerical score never overrides a failed critical evidence gate.

## Evidence states
- VERIFIED
- REPORTED_BY_SOURCE
- UNVERIFIED
- MISSING
- CONTRADICTED

Missing evidence is a state, not an invitation to infer.

## Phase 2 production gate
No production certification until a representative real-world corpus is defined, sourced and tested across project classes, jurisdictions, risk patterns and buyer/investor use cases. The acceptance corpus and gate thresholds must be approved before certification.

## Relationship to Phase 1
Phase 1 remains the distressed-property auction foundation.

Phase 2 shares the common Shakti governance/evidence principles but has project-specific schemas, gates, workflows and acceptance tests.
