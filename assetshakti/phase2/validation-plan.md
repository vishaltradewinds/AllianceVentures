# AssetShakti Phase 2 — Validation Plan

## Current state
Validation corpus: 10 cases across Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra and Gujarat, including a negative control.

## Required expansion before production certification
Minimum target: **50 real new-project cases** across:
- at least 5 States/UTs
- residential, commercial, industrial, mixed-use, plotted development and other material project classes
- early-stage, active construction, delayed/lapsed/abeyance/revoked and completed states
- multiple promoter/entity structures
- meaningful title/approval/litigation/encumbrance/economic risk patterns
- positive, conditional, insufficient-evidence and negative controls

## Acceptance criteria
1. Every case has authoritative source references.
2. Every material assertion has an evidence status.
3. State-specific status labels are preserved.
4. RERA registration never automatically produces BUY_READY or INVESTMENT_READY.
5. Applicable critical evidence missing or contradicted cannot produce a ready decision.
6. Revoked/lapsed/abeyance/withdrawn/deregistered cases are explicitly distinguished.
7. Contradictions remain visible until resolved.
8. Decisions are reproducible from the evidence ledger.
9. AI-generated assertions never become VERIFIED without source evidence.
10. Jurisdiction-specific rules are not generalized across States/UTs.
11. Human review is mandatory for ambiguous critical evidence.
12. Production certification occurs only after corpus review and signed gate acceptance.
