# AssetShakti External Evidence Request Pack — 2026-10-10

## Purpose and operating rule
This pack makes the remaining external evidence requests actionable. It does not claim that any request was sent, that a source responded, or that an auction outcome is known. Send each request only through the contact details published by the relevant liquidator or official BAANKNET case page. Do not submit bids, EMD, KYC, or binding declarations as part of evidence collection.

## Common request (copy and send separately for each pilot)
**Subject:** Request for current-round auction records and post-auction status — [Corporate Debtor] — [Auction date]

Dear Liquidator / Authorised Auction Contact,

We are conducting a documentary review of the liquidation auction for **[Corporate Debtor]**, intended round dated **[Auction date]**, reference/lot **[exact reference if available]**. Please provide or identify the official source for:

1. The original E-Auction Process Information Document applicable to this exact round and each annexure/data-room index referenced by it.
2. Every corrigendum, addendum, extension, revised notice or withdrawal issued for this round, with publication dates and confirmation of which version controls.
3. The official current status and outcome: held/postponed/cancelled/withdrawn/sold/unsold/re-auctioned, with the date and the official record supporting that status. If completed, please confirm whether a sale certificate or sale confirmation was issued; please do not disclose confidential bidder information.
4. The exact lot/component list and the legal interest offered (freehold, leasehold, plant/machinery, going concern, composite lot or other), including exclusions and assets not owned by the corporate debtor.
5. The documents governing title, possession, encumbrances, litigation, access, utilities, taxes/dues, statutory approvals, environmental liabilities, lease assignment/lessor consent, employee liabilities, and buyer-side transfer/compliance costs, as applicable.
6. The inspection and data-room access process and any remaining permitted access window.

Please confirm in writing if any requested record does not exist or is no longer available, and direct us to the authoritative public URL or portal listing. We will verify document identity and integrity before relying on any record. This request is for due diligence only and is not an offer or bid.

Regards,
AssetShakti evidence review
