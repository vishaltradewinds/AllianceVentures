# Phase 1 Candidate Verification Checklist

A candidate auction record becomes eligible for the 50-case certification corpus only after a reviewer records all of the following:

1. Corporate debtor name matches the authoritative IBBI listing.
2. Auction date matches the authoritative IBBI listing.
3. Reserve price matches the authoritative IBBI listing.
4. Asset nature/classification matches the authoritative IBBI listing.
5. Source URL resolves to the authoritative IBBI record.
6. Underlying auction notice is identified where deeper evidence is required.
7. Any correction/corrigendum is linked and supersedes stale listing data.
8. The observation date is recorded.
9. The record is assigned a sourceVerificationStatus of VERIFIED.
10. No title, possession, valuation, encumbrance or litigation conclusion is promoted without its own evidence.

A candidate that fails any of these checks remains CANDIDATE_DISCOVERY_PENDING_VERIFICATION and does not count toward production certification.
