## Evidence rule

Historical documents may be used as historical evidence, but their bidder obligations must not be carried into a later auction round by inference.

## Current decision

Production certification: OFF.

No pilot may become BID_READY until:
1. exact current document version is identified;
2. current/corrigendum versions are reconciled;
3. bidder obligations are source-traceable to the applicable version;
4. title/interest, possession, encumbrance, litigation, physical/location, valuation and provenance evidence are separately evaluated.

## Verified historical example

Vysali's 17-03-2026 third auction notice states sale on an "as is where is, as is what is" basis and identifies separate Edathala land/building, Edappally land/building and Edathala plant/machinery parcels. This is historical evidence only and is not substituted for the 10-10-2026 current round.

## Shakti principle

Missing evidence remains missing. A verified IBBI listing does not imply verified title, possession, valuation or bidder obligations.
