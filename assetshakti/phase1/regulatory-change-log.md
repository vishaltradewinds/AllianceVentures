# AssetShakti Phase 1 — Regulatory Change Log

## 2026-10-06

### Change-control observation
Recent Indian insolvency jurisprudence has confirmed that auction-specific terms can materially affect bidder exposure, including forfeiture of EMD/amounts paid when the auction notice contains an applicable forfeiture condition. AssetShakti therefore treats auction notice terms as source-traceable decision evidence rather than assuming the generic regulation alone determines bidder exposure.

### Implementation rule
1. Preserve the exact auction notice/corrigendum source.
2. Capture EMD, payment deadlines, extension/interest terms, forfeiture language, eligibility and sale-basis language.
3. Record observation date and source version.
4. A later regulation, circular, court order or corrigendum must not silently mutate an earlier case.
5. Re-evaluation is required when a material regulatory or auction-term change affects an active decision.

This is governance/change-control evidence, not legal advice or a title/rights determination.
