# Shakti Standards — External Reference Ecosystem Extension

**Status:** Normative Shakti Standards extension  
**Date:** 2026-11-08

## Purpose

Shakti Standards shall use proven external innovation, open-AI, digital-public-good and digital-public-infrastructure ecosystems as reference inputs across the full project lifecycle.

These ecosystems are **reference systems, not authorities to copy wholesale**. Any adoption must be independently validated for Indian statutory/regulatory applicability, licensing, security, privacy, interoperability, operational fitness, provenance and evidence requirements.

## Mandatory Reference Ecosystems

1. **UN Innovation Network (UNIN)**
   - Innovation practice, institutional adoption, scaling, portfolios and systems thinking.
   - Use to assess movement from isolated pilots/projects toward repeatable operating capabilities and scalable portfolios.

2. **FAIR Forward — Artificial Intelligence for All**
   - Open-source AI, open data, local-language AI, responsible AI, inclusive digital ecosystems and capacity building.
   - Use to identify reusable AI/open-data patterns and country-relevant responsible-AI practices.

3. **Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA)**
   - Digital public goods discovery, reuse and open-source ecosystem patterns.
   - Use to identify potentially reusable software, standards, datasets, AI models and other open components.

4. **Global DPI Repository**
   - Proven digital public infrastructure implementations, governance and deployment patterns.
   - Use for comparative architecture, governance, maturity and implementation analysis.

5. **Relevant Indian DPI ecosystems**
   - Use Indian national interoperability, identity, language, data-exchange and public digital infrastructure patterns where legally and technically applicable.
   - Indian statutory and regulatory requirements remain controlling over external reference models.

## Shakti Operating Rule

**Reference → Cross-map → Validate → Adapt → Implement → Verify → Assure.**

No external ecosystem, framework, component or architecture may be represented as part of a project's implemented capability merely because it is identified as a reference.

## Required Cross-Mapping

For every material project, Shakti Standards shall maintain an evidence-backed crosswalk covering:

- purpose and problem addressed;
- architecture and interfaces;
- open-source/open-standard status;
- licence and reuse conditions;
- data provenance;
- security and privacy implications;
- interoperability;
- responsible-AI requirements where applicable;
- governance and institutional ownership;
- deployment/maturity evidence;
- Indian statutory/regulatory compatibility;
- operational dependencies;
- cost and sustainability;
- adaptation required;
- actual project implementation status;
- verification/evidence;
- residual gaps and risks.

## Evidence and Claim Discipline

Shakti Standards shall explicitly distinguish:

- **Reference capability** — exists in an external ecosystem.
- **Adopted component** — selected for use after validation.
- **Implemented capability** — actually integrated into the project.
- **Verified capability** — tested with evidence.
- **Operational capability** — proven in the intended operating environment.
- **Certified/regulatory status** — established only by the competent authority or applicable formal process.

External reference material must never be used to inflate TRL, production readiness, regulatory compliance, certification or field validation.

## RupayKg Application

For RupayKg, the reference layer shall be used to inform:

**UNIN** → innovation scaling and institutional adoption  
**FAIR Forward** → open AI, responsible AI and inclusive/local-language patterns  
**DPGA** → reusable digital public goods  
**Global DPI Repository** → proven DPI architecture and deployment patterns  
**Indian DPI ecosystems** → India-specific interoperability and national-scale infrastructure patterns

These inputs shall then be reconciled against:

**Indian environmental law/regulation → CPCB/EPR → CCTS/BEE where applicable → MRV/evidence requirements → field instrumentation → security → data governance → operational acceptance.**

## Golden Rule

> **ONE SHAKTI STANDARD. MANY PROJECT-SPECIFIC OPERATING SYSTEMS.**

External ecosystems strengthen the reference and engineering layer; they do not replace project-specific statutory compliance, engineering validation, field evidence, operational acceptance or independent assurance.
